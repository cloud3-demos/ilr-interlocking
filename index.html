Why the base matters

Two patios can look the same the week they’re finished, and the difference shows a few winters later.

Ontario’s freeze and thaw is what lifts and sinks interlock that was laid on a thin base. Every ILR job starts underneath the stone:

  • Excavation to the right depth for patios, walks or driveways
  • Grading that moves water away from your walls
  • A compacted base and edge restraint before any stone goes down
  • Steps and landings sized to stay comfortable and drained

Questions

Common questions about interlock work

Do you take on smaller jobs, like a single walkway or steps?

Yes. Targeted upgrades like a walkway, front steps or a patio are a big part of what we do, and they can be planned so a bigger project fits around them later.

How do you price an interlock patio?

On what it actually takes to get it right: site conditions, access, excavation and base, materials, labour and finish. We visit the site first, then give you a written quote broken down that way.

How long does a patio or walkway take?

Smaller jobs can run a few days, and larger projects longer. We confirm dates in writing after the site visit, once we know the scope and conditions.

My patio or walkway is sinking. Can you fix it?

Usually, yes. Sinking and pooling tend to come from the base or the grading underneath, so we correct that first and relay the stone on top.

What areas do you serve?

Mississauga, Brampton, Etobicoke, Caledon and Toronto, plus other GTA properties depending on the project. Send your address with the form and we’ll confirm.
